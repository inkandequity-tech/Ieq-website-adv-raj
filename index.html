Pregnancy and public jobs: the Allahabad High Court on Komal Jaiswal v. State of U.P.

A Division Bench of the Lucknow Bench held that a candidate who is nine months pregnant cannot be refused a postponed physical test only because the rules are silent. A reading of the judgment of 22 July 2026.
