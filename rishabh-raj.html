Background

After law school at Symbiosis in Noida, Rishabh Raj worked in corporate legal roles before enrolling in 2018. He then returned to Lucknow, trained in civil practice and, in 2021 to 2022, set up Rishabh Raj & Associates. The practice became Ink & Equity on 29 March 2024. Read the history of the practice.

A note on names. More than one advocate in India is called Rishabh Raj. This page is about the advocate who practises in Lucknow at Ink & Equity, 1, Moti Mahal Marg, Hazratganj.
