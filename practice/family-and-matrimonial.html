Areas of practice

Family and matrimonial matters

Divorce, maintenance, custody, domestic violence and other family proceedings, handled with care for everyone affected, including children.

General information, last reviewed 14 October 2026.

What this covers

  • Divorce by mutual consent and contested divorce.
  • Judicial separation, nullity and restitution of conjugal rights, where relevant.
  • Maintenance and interim maintenance.
  • Child custody, guardianship and visitation.
  • Protection orders, residence orders and other relief under the Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, 2005.
  • Division of matrimonial property and financial disclosures.

Governing law

Matters are governed by personal laws (such as the Hindu Marriage Act, 1955 and the Muslim Personal Law as applicable) and the Special Marriage Act, 1954. Custody and guardianship are governed by the Guardians and Wards Act, 1890 and the relevant personal law. Maintenance may also be claimed under the provisions of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023.

Sensitivity

Family matters involve children and private information. Content on this site, and any filing, avoids identifying children and keeps personal details confidential.

Common questions

Can a divorce be obtained by mutual consent?

Under the Hindu Marriage Act, a mutual consent divorce generally involves a waiting period after the first petition. The law must be checked for the parties' personal law.

Does the Domestic Violence Act apply only to married women?

The Act provides relief to women in domestic relationships. Whether it applies depends on the relationship and facts.
