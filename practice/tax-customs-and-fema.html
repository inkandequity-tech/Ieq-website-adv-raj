Tax, customs and FEMA

Responding to tax notices and appeals, customs and GST disputes, and foreign exchange compliance for businesses and individuals.

General information, last reviewed 13 October 2026.

What this covers

  • Income-tax notices, assessments, penalties and appeals.
  • GST registration, classification, input tax credit disputes, refunds and appeals.
  • Customs classification, valuation and duty disputes.
  • FEMA and Reserve Bank of India compliance for inward and outward remittances, and foreign investment reporting.

Scope note

Tax planning and tax computation are handled with qualified tax professionals. This practice does not promise tax outcomes. Tax and FEMA positions depend on the year, the facts and the current law, which must be checked before advice is given.

Time limits

Notices carry deadlines for reply and appeal, and missing them can have serious consequences. Dates on any notice should be recorded at once.

Common questions

What should I do on receiving a tax notice?

Record the date, note the deadline for reply, and seek advice before responding. Do not ignore the notice.

Can GST input tax credit be claimed on every purchase?

Only where the conditions in the GST law are met. The conditions depend on the facts.
