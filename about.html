About

A practice that began as one advocate's chamber.

Ink & Equity is a law practice in Lucknow, formerly Rishabh Raj & Associates. It took its present name on 29 March 2024.

The name

Ink is the written record: the petition, the affidavit, the order. Much of litigation is decided on paper, and the practice treats drafting as the core of the work.

Equity is the older idea behind it: that the law should be applied with attention to what is fair in the case in front of the court.

How the practice grew

  1. 2018

    Enrolment and civil practice

    Rishabh Raj enrolled with the Bar Council of Uttar Pradesh and began practice in Lucknow in the chambers of Shri Brijesh Kumar Saxena, appearing in civil courts.

  2. 2021 to 2022

    Rishabh Raj & Associates

    With guidance from Shri Ram Raj Singh, advocate, he set up his own practice. Work widened to constitutional, service and family matters.

  3. 2023 to 2024

    Vedant Bhargav joins

    Vedant Bhargav joined the practice, adding family, criminal and contract matters.

  4. 29 March 2024

    Ink & Equity

    The practice took its present name.
