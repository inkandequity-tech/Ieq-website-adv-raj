Before a first meeting

A few things make a first meeting more useful.

  1. Call or write

    Say what the matter is about and, if a case is pending, the court and the next date.

  2. Gather the papers

    Orders, notices, agreements and receipts, in date order. Copies are enough.

  3. Meet the advocate

    The advocate explains the forum, the time limits and the options.

  4. Decide

    If you choose to engage the advocate, the terms are agreed in writing.

Advocates in India may not solicit work. This page lists contact details for people who have chosen to get in touch. It is not an invitation to instruct, and writing to the chamber does not by itself create an advocate-client relationship.
