Privacy

What this site keeps.

This site has no forms, accounts, analytics or advertising. It does not set cookies.

In your browser

Your browser may store two small settings on your own device: your light or dark theme choice, and whether you have dismissed the notice at the bottom of the page. They are never sent to us.

On the server

The site is delivered through third-party hosting. Like most hosts, they may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested. We do not control those logs.

If you write or call

If you send an email or call the chamber, the details you give are used to reply to you and are kept confidential. Please do not send documents until an advocate has asked for them.

Changes

If the site starts using analytics or other tools, this page will be updated first.
